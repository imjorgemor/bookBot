1. Open **Claude Code Desktop** (any folder is fine, e.g. your Documents folder).
2. Copy everything in the box below, paste it into Claude and press Enter.
3. Follow Claude's instructions. It will ask you to do a few things in your browser and do the rest itself. About 30 minutes.
